Hi-Fi Spacesuit

MIT Space Architecture Spacesuit Group Project

Group project, MAS.S66/4.154/16.89 Space Architecture, MIT Master of Architecture

Course overview and instructors

Team members: Mateo, xdd, Farida, Nara

Exhibited in summer 2026 at Life in Space, Exploratorium, San Francisco

Bones and muscles grow weak in the weightlessness of space. One way to counteract this is vibration therapy-stimulating bone and muscle cells by shaking them. This suit, designed to be worn inside a space station or habitat, features strategically placed vibrating pads that deliver vibration therapy to key muscle groups and bones.
